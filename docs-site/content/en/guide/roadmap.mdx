None of this is in the mod today. Details will change as it gets built.

## Survival

- **Spool winder**: winds copper onto empty spools, so cable costs something. Until it's in, the spool comes full from its recipe.
- Recipes that fit a survival progression. Some of the current ones are placeholders.

## Data bus

- **Wireless towers and antennas**, to reach machines without running cable all the way.
- A **focus mode** for placing ports precisely on crowded faces.
- The editor's **Flows** panel, showing what moves between machines and programs, live.

## Screens and panels

- **Drawing on Brass Screens** from programs.
- Wiring the **panel controls** to programs: buttons, switches, knobs and sliders to read; lamps, nixie digits and gauges to drive.

## Other mods

- **Linked Typewriter** integration.
- The **Bearing Servo** for Create: Aeronautics ships: turn a contraption to an exact angle on command.
