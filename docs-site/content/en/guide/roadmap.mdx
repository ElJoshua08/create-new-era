None of this is in the mod today. Details will change as it gets built. What has already shipped is in each version's [release notes](https://github.com/ElJoshua08/create-new-era/releases).

## Survival

- **Spool winder**: winds copper onto empty spools, so cable costs something. Until it's in, the spool comes full from its recipe.
- Recipes that fit a survival progression. The current ones work, but they will change. See [Recipes](./recipes.mdx).

## Computer

- **Keeping files** when you break a computer, or moving them to another one. Today breaking it loses its disk.
- **Redstone events**: a program that waits for a signal still checks once a tick. See [Redstone](./redstone.mdx#waiting-for-a-signal).

## Editor

- The **Flows** panel, showing what moves between machines and programs, live. Its tab is there, but it only says "Coming soon".

## Data bus

- **Wireless towers and antennas**, to reach machines without running cable all the way.
- A **focus mode** for placing ports precisely on crowded faces.
- **Renaming devices** in game. Names are still the block id plus a number. See [The data bus](./bus.mdx).
- The **Universal Adapter**: it can be made and placed, but does nothing yet. A [data port](./bus.mdx) does its job.

## Screens and panels

- The **pixel shape** option: screens don't read it yet, so every dot is square. See [Config](./config.mdx).

## Other mods

- The **Bearing Servo** for Create: Aeronautics ships: turn a contraption to an exact angle on command. It can be made and placed, but isn't wired up yet. Create's own bearings already take orders from programs; see [Devices](./devices.mdx).
